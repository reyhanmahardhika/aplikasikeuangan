# Keuangan AI — UI/UX Redesign

## Visual direction

Editorial fintech: calm, structured, and data-first. The interface uses warm off-white surfaces, charcoal for financial hierarchy, emerald as the product color, and lime only for high-intent actions.

## Interaction rules

- Lime is reserved for the most important action or active navigation state.
- Green communicates product identity and positive financial states.
- Rose and amber remain semantic colors for expense, risk, and warnings.
- Cards use restrained borders and low elevation; hierarchy comes from spacing and typography.
- Mobile navigation keeps five clear destinations and gives Copilot a distinct central treatment.
- Forms use consistent 44px controls, visible focus states, and concise labels.
- Loading, empty, and error states share the same visual language as the product surfaces.

## Coverage

The redesign applies to the application shell, desktop sidebar, desktop header, mobile top bar, mobile navigation, dashboard, authentication, transaction flows, pocket management, shared pockets, categories, budgets, schedules, reports, Finance Copilot, notifications, profile, modals, public pocket history, and reusable loading/empty/error states.
